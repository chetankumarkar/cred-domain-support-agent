# Loan eligibility by product
Personal loan applicants should be adults with verifiable income and a repayment capacity that meets the lender's affordability review. Home-loan applicants need documented income, property details, and a satisfactory title and valuation review. Auto-loan applicants provide vehicle and dealer information alongside identity and income evidence. Education-loan applications include course, institution, fee schedule, and student/co-applicant details. Business-loan applications require business vintage, cash-flow evidence, ownership details, and applicable registrations. Eligibility is assessed individually; these guideposts do not promise approval.
